# Exam attempts do not update FSRS

Exam Mode may intentionally test Concepts or Cards before their FSRS due dates, so its results are stored as Exam Attempts rather than Card reviews. Exam Attempts may contribute Needs Work Signals and Concept Learning State evidence, but they do not change Card Memory State, due dates, or FSRS history. A due Card can be explicitly sent to normal Review Mode if the student wants to complete a scheduling review.
