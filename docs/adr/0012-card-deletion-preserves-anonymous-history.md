# Card deletion preserves anonymous review history

Delete Card removes the Card block from Markdown and clears its active FSRS state and queue controls, but keeps a content-free Card Tombstone and historical review events. Deleted Cards do not contribute to Concept Learning State, and their immutable Card IDs are never reused. A separate explicit `Delete History Too` action permanently removes the tombstone and review history when the student wants complete erasure; Archive Card remains the safer user-facing choice when content should be preserved. Its persisted state keeps the historical `retiredCards` name for compatibility.

When `Delete Card` is used on the current Card in an active review session, Mneme removes that Card from the in-memory session after the Markdown deletion and tombstone update succeed. The Card that shifts into the current position is shown next without returning to Today's Focus. If no unreviewed Card remains in the current Concept, Mneme shows the existing Review Complete state and refreshes the review queue in the background. A deletion is reported separately from reviewed, skipped, deferred, and suspended Cards in the completion summary. Deletion from diagnostics may still perform a full queue refresh because it is outside an active recall loop.
