# Decouple Concept importance from FSRS retention

Concept Importance expresses the student's judgment of knowledge value, while Retention Target expresses a workload-versus-recall scheduling policy. Mneme therefore uses Importance only to rank already-eligible Concepts and does not automatically map importance levels to FSRS desired retention. FSRS keeps a global Retention Target, with any future Concept-specific override requiring an explicit user choice rather than a silent consequence of editing Importance.
