# AI knowledge changes require individual review

Mneme does not permit bulk acceptance of AI proposals that the student has not individually reviewed in full. Concept proposals must expose their proposed meaning, views, target, and evidence; Card proposals must expose Front, Back, and Rubric. Confidence never substitutes for approval. Mneme may support bulk rejection or deferral and efficient keyboard navigation, but every accepted knowledge change must pass the Review Gate before Markdown is written.
