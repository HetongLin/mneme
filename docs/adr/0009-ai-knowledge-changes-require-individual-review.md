# AI knowledge changes require individual review

Mneme does not permit bulk acceptance of AI proposals that the learner has not individually reviewed in full. Concept proposals must expose their proposed meaning, views, target, and evidence; Card proposals must expose Front, Back, and Rubric. Confidence never substitutes for approval. Mneme may support bulk rejection or deferral and efficient keyboard navigation, but every accepted knowledge change must pass the Review Gate before Markdown is written.

The Inbox list itself is not the Review Gate. It is only a queue of pending proposal titles. A learner may open a proposal or reject it from the queue, but cannot accept it there.

The Review Gate is the full proposal detail surface. It must show editable Concept or Card content before acceptance, followed by concrete source evidence excerpts that justify the proposal. `Proposed Change` summaries are not required for Concept proposals when they duplicate editable fields. Evidence must be readable enough for the learner to judge whether the AI extraction is grounded; an evidence count alone is not sufficient.

The detail surface may keep raw JSON as an Advanced escape hatch, but acceptance must remain a bottom action after the learner has had access to the full editable content and evidence.
