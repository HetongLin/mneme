# Concept merge requires guided review

AI may flag a Possible Duplicate, but Mneme does not treat merging two written Concepts as an ordinary one-click Inbox acceptance. A merge must run as a Guided Merge that makes the surviving identity, content reconciliation, Source links, Cards, FSRS history, and affected Markdown visible before the student confirms the final diff. This preserves the approval boundary while still letting AI accelerate duplicate discovery.

The student chooses one surviving Concept, which keeps its identity and path. The merged Concept becomes a Redirect Note so existing Obsidian links continue to resolve, while its Cards retain their stable IDs and FSRS history when moved into the surviving Card Group. Source links and diagnostic signals are migrated and deduplicated as part of the reviewed merge.
